## Releases
[NetBSD 10.2 released](https://blog.netbsd.org/tnf/entry/netbsd_10_2_released?utm_source=bsdweekly): It represents a selected subset of fixes deemed important for security or stability reasons since the release of NetBSD 10.1 in December 2024. It is fully compatible with NetBSD 10.0.
This release includes security updates for ipfilter, telnet, kernel TCP support, OpenSSL, Unbound, and X.Org. It improves the stability of the iwm(4) Wi-Fi driver, and fixes support for Olimex A64 and MIPS R5000SC systems. Newly available is the ability to boot an amd64 kernel directly with QEMU's -kernel option.

[ekkoBSD 2.1 Released](https://ekkobsd.org/news/1?utm_source=bsdweekly): After more than two decades of dormancy, ekkoBSD has returned with release 2.1delivering a small, auditable 4.4BSD-derived operating system for amd64 featuring a modern build system, updated handbook, and 742 native .mom packages.

## News
[Statement of the FreeBSD Core Team Regarding the EU Cyber Resilience Act](https://bsdsec.net/articles/statement-of-the-freebsd-core-team-regarding-the-eu-cyber-resilience-act?utm_source=bsdweekly): The FreeBSD Core Team formally endorses the FreeBSD Foundation as an open-source software steward for EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) compliance, while affirming that downstream enterprise partners and contributors may also independently fulfill steward roles.

[Valuable News – 2026/09/21](https://vermaden.wordpress.com/2026/09/21/valuable-news-2026-09-21/?utm_source=bsdweekly): vermaden's weekly BSD digest covering Apiary (a Bhyve/Jails cluster platform), FreeBSD on Framework Laptop, NetBSD GSoC Enlightenment E26 progress, GEFS on OpenBSD, and EuroBSDCon 2026 video releases.

[NetBSD GSoC 2026: Enlightenment Progress Update 2](https://blog.netbsd.org/tnf/entry/gsoc2026_enlightenment_2?utm_source=bsdweekly): The second status report on bringing the Enlightenment desktop to NetBSD details hardware-accelerated DRM/DRI fixes in EFL, BSD socket fixes in Ecore_Con for X11 and Wayland compositing, and updated packages in pkgsrc-wip.

[BSD Now 681: UNIX still powering the worlds infrastructure](https://www.bsdnow.tv/681?utm_source=bsdweekly): Episode 681 of BSD Now discusses where Unix runs today, WebZFS updates, NetBSD 11 support, Sylve for FreeBSD bhyve virtualization with Ansible, a remote filesystem for 2.11BSD, and more.

[FreeBSD: The Hidden Cost of Upgrading in Production](https://klarasystems.com/articles/freebsd-the-hidden-cost-of-upgrading-in-production/?utm_source=bsdweekly): Klara Systems breaks down the hidden costs of upgrading FreeBSD in production, covering engineering time, testing, package changes, and compliance risks. It outlines practical strategies to minimize friction, including ZFS boot environments, staged canary rollouts, and poudriere package builds.

[EuroBSDCon 2026 Talks Online](https://www.undeadly.org/cgi?action=article;sid=20260916093106&utm_source=bsdweekly): Video recordings from EuroBSDCon 2026 in Brussels are now available, featuring OpenBSD presentations on pkg_add updates, the errata process, routing domains for VPNs, input device handling, and more.

[GEFS on OpenBSD: A Very Early Preview](https://marc.info/?l=openbsd-tech&m=178948744271633&w=2&utm_source=bsdweekly): Ori Bernstein has published an early preview port of GEFS to OpenBSD, bringing the copy-on-write, snapshotting filesystem originally written for 9front into the kernel. The implementation is under 9,000 lines of code and is not yet production-ready, with remaining work focused on error handling, userspace tools, and POSIX compatibility.
